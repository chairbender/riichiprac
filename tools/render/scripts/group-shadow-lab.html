Group shadow lab

Each card draws the same zone twice from the manifest: live is the proposed UI recipe — hard rounded-rect footprints at full alpha, one CSS filter: blur(σ) on the group, then opacity: coreAlpha — and the reference PNG is the Phase 1d recipe (blur each footprint, take the per-pixel max) composed by preview-group-shadow.mjs.

The bodies are the committed blank shells (no face atlas yet), at their real gridStepPx / face-centre steps. The felt is the committed raster.
