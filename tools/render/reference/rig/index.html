v4-shadow-decal — v4 soft gradient hand shadow

v4 keeps v3's spacing (the player's hand at 9.3) and replaces the rendered physical-pose caster with a fake soft gradient decal: the v3 caster read as hard black rectangles that started at the rear edge where the tile balances. The decal is a straight-alpha black strip laid on the mat whose darkest point is hidden under the tile and whose visible gradient begins right at the tile's front edge, as if the tile sat upright and level, then fades to nothing within a third of a tile height. It casts no shadow rays, so the wall behind stays clean.

v8-shadow-blurred-footprint — v8 blurred-footprint hand shadow (take A + Gaussian blob)

Take A (v5) rebuilt with the retired UI shadow recipe: a rounded contact rectangle, blurred once, drawn under the hand. One plane per hand group spans its footprint plus a 2.5 sigma margin; the alpha is 0.5*maxAlpha at the footprint's front and end edges and falls to zero two sigma outside. That puts the shadow's first visible pixels exactly on the front edge and wraps it around the row's left and right ends, fixing the strip's horizontal fade-in. No shadow rays, so the wall behind stays clean.
