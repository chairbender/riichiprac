Carved face tuner

Preview renders only the upright 1m decal at 2×, fast samples and a coarse carve grid. Apply writes into scene-spec.json; run bash tools/render/render.sh for the full set.

Parameters

Recolours the red fills only; the black bar and the internal shading are preserved.
Fraction of the 16.5 mm thickness, fine steps at the shallow end. 0.08 mm is 0.005.
Distance from the lip to the flat floor. Larger lets the curvature run further into the bottom.
Bézier control where the face rolls into the well. 0 = none, 1 = widest/softest.
Bézier control where the wall curves into the floor. Drives the glossy highlight.
Smoothing of the symbol edge. Also caps how wide the transition can be.
Where the paint starts inside the transition. 0 = at the lip, 1 = only at the floor.
Render cost only; denoised either way.
Lighting
Azimuth is around the tile: 0° front, 90° right, 180° behind, 270° left. Elevation is degrees above horizontal; a top-lit upright tile wants the key near 0° / 70–80°.
